The pipeline

Each stage is separate, and each is auditable.

1. Collect

Public construction and permit records are read from City sources. Every response is stored verbatim before it is parsed, so a correction can be replayed without re-requesting from the City.

2. Normalize

Source records are mapped onto a common shape. Values a source does not publish stay empty. Nothing is inferred to fill a gap.

3. Assemble

Individual permits at one address are grouped into the underlying project, so a building with mechanical, electrical and plumbing permits appears once, not three times.

4. Attach evidence

Every field is recorded with the source that supplied it, the record identifier, the source's own date, and the excerpt that supports it.

5. Classify

Projects are scored against documented evidence and labelled HIGH, MEDIUM or NEEDS_VERIFICATION. Every point awarded is recorded as a readable reason.

6. Report

Verified projects become opportunity pages and customer reports. Missing information is shown as Not verified rather than estimated or omitted.

Why two tiers of mechanical evidence

Tier 1 — mechanical permit on file
The building department filed a mechanical permit at the address. The City itself recorded mechanical scope, so no interpretation is needed.
Tier 2 — mechanical scope in permit text
The recorded work description names mechanical scope. This is documentary evidence from the permit text rather than a separate trade permit.

A project cannot reach HIGH on property class and value alone. Mechanical scope must be documented.

What we do not do

  • We do not claim a project is an open bid. No source in this market publishes bid status.
  • We do not estimate a project value, floor area, owner, contractor or architect. If the source does not publish it, we show Not verified.
  • We do not list finished work as an active opportunity.
  • We do not treat service, repair, maintenance or like-for-like replacement as a construction project.
  • We do not rank opportunities by an invented attractiveness score.

Important

Permit evidence does not confirm that a project is currently accepting bids for any trade. Confirm procurement directly with the owner or general contractor, and confirm all figures with the issuing jurisdiction before commercial reliance.
