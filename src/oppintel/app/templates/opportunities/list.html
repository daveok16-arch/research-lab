Listings include projects classified HIGH or MEDIUM with a mechanical or HVAC evidence tier. Work recorded as finished or inactive is excluded from discovery, and can still be reached by its direct link, where its status is shown.

Results are ordered by a factual key only — permit date, update time or project status. There is no subjective "best opportunity" ranking.
