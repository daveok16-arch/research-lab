What the product does

Six things, each grounded in a public record rather than an estimate.

Commercial project discovery

Individual permits are assembled into the underlying commercial projects, so you see one project rather than eighteen separate trade permits.

Mechanical / HVAC evidence

Each project states exactly what triggered its mechanical classification: a mechanical permit filed at the address, or mechanical scope named in the permit text.

Project status

Where the source records a permit status, we show it — plan review, issued, under inspection — and we exclude work that is already finished.

Source verification

Every material fact links back to the public record it came from. If a source does not publish a field, we say Not verified rather than filling it in.

Procurement transparency

We state the procurement status plainly. No source in this market publishes bid status, so no project is ever presented as an open bid.

Search and filtering

Filter by city, project type, classification, procurement status and date range, or search by address, project name or permit number.

Recently filed opportunities

Ordered by permit date. There is no "best opportunity" ranking, because that would be a judgement the records do not support.

How to read this data

A mechanical permit on file shows that mechanical work was filed with the City at that address. It is evidence of scope. It is not a guarantee that the work is still available, and it does not indicate who will perform it.

Data was last collected in {{ freshness.display }}. Coverage is not complete for every jurisdiction in the market, and some source record types are capped by a pagination safety limit, so some totals are lower bounds.
